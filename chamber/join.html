Non-Profit (NP) Membership

Annual Fee: ₦0 (Verification of non-profit status required)

  • Listing in the online Chamber Member Directory
  • Access to monthly community networking mixers
  • Eligibility to post volunteer and community drives on the Chamber board
  • Quarterly economic & civic policy briefings

Bronze Membership

Annual Fee: ₦75,000 / year

  • All Non-Profit tier directory and networking benefits
  • Ribbon-cutting ceremony announcement for new locations
  • 10% discount on Chamber workshops and business clinics
  • Member-to-member marketplace discount privileges

Silver Membership

Annual Fee: ₦180,000 / year

  • All Bronze tier benefits included
  • 25% discount on all Chamber training sessions, summits, and trade expos
  • Bi-monthly featured business spotlight in the Chamber newsletter
  • Complimentary access to two executive leadership roundtables per year

Gold Membership

Annual Fee: ₦350,000 / year

  • All Silver tier benefits included
  • Rotating homepage spotlight placement on the Chamber website
  • 50% discount on all event sponsorships, booths, and training programs
  • VIP invitations to annual Governor & Industry Policy Luncheons
  • Dedicated promotional feature across Chamber social channels
